## Standup Guidelines

### Do's
- ✅ Be on time and prepared
- ✅ Keep updates brief and relevant
- ✅ Focus on sprint goals
- ✅ Report blockers immediately
- ✅ Take detailed discussions offline

### Don'ts
- ❌ Go over 15 minutes
- ❌ Deep-dive into technical details
- ❌ Report to manager (report to team)
- ❌ Miss standup without notice
- ❌ Multitask during standup

### Virtual Standup Etiquette
- Camera on when possible
- Mute when not speaking
- Use "raise hand" feature for questions
- Post updates in chat if joining late
